Privacy Policy

At A&K Consultancy, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information in connection with our services, website, and communications.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you contact us, request a quote, send an inquiry, or engage with our services. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, location, and any information you provide in your message or project request.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services
  • Manage projects and client communications
  • Improve our service quality and client experience
  • Send operational updates or relevant information regarding your project
  • Meet legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share personal data only when necessary to deliver services, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or work with trusted service providers who support our operations under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information using administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. While no system is completely risk-free, we aim to maintain a secure environment for information entrusted to us.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand visitor activity, improve user experience, and gather non-identifying information about traffic and usage patterns. You may disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where legally applicable. If you would like to update your preferences or ask questions about your data, please contact us using the details below.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at hello@ak-consultancy.online.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.
